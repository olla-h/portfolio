# E-Livery — Escrow, Delivery & BNPL

## Overview

E-Livery was a product concept combining escrow, delivery, and Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) capabilities, with a focus on Nordic markets.

## Concept

The service aimed to improve trust and transaction convenience by combining payment protection with delivery and flexible payment functionality.

- Product concept development
- Business-model thinking
- Market-oriented analysis
- Service design

- Team-based project coordination
