# PawPerFit — Pet E-commerce

## Overview

PawPerFit was an e-commerce project focused on pet products and the digital customer journey.

## Work

- E-commerce concept development
- Product/service planning
- Customer-oriented thinking
- Digital marketing considerations
- Market and competitor analysis
- Project documentation

## Tools / Areas

E-commerce, digital marketing, analytics, Canva, Google Forms, market research

## Outcome

The project provided hands-on experience connecting technology, analytics, marketing, and customer experience within an e-commerce context.
